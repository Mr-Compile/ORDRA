# Ordra Branding Bible

## Canonical identity
- Product name: **Ordra**
- Platform: **Android only**
- Tagline: **All your orders. One place.**
- Brand idea: a calm, intelligent personal order hub for purchases from Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and any other seller.

## Visual direction
Ordra uses a modern productivity/shopping identity rather than copying marketplace branding. The primary identity is deep indigo with electric violet, supported by mint for successful/delivered states and amber for attention/COD reminders.

## Status semantics
Use status colors as chips, icons, indicators, or small accents. Do not flood entire cards with status colors.

Order status:
- Ordered: Indigo
- Processing: Violet
- Packed: Blue
- Shipped: Cyan
- Delivered: Mint/Green
- Cancelled: Red
- Returned: Orange
- Refunded: Teal

Shipment status (parcel movement): Preparing/Picked Up: Violet; In Transit: Cyan;
Arrived at Hub: Blue; Out for Delivery: Amber; Delivered: Mint/Green;
Delivery Failed/Returning: warm accent; Returned: Orange; Unknown: neutral.

## Logo direction
The primary symbol should combine an **O** with a parcel/order cue and a subtle check/route concept. Keep geometry simple enough to remain recognizable as an Android launcher icon. Avoid generic standalone cardboard-box artwork.

Included SVGs are starter concepts, not immutable final artwork. The team may iterate on them while preserving the Ordra identity.

## Splash direction
Use the Ordra symbol, wordmark, and tagline on a clean light or dark background. Avoid long splash animations. Android system splash requirements must be respected.

## UI principles
1. Material 3 foundation with a distinct Ordra identity.
2. Clean surfaces and strong information hierarchy.
3. Light and dark themes are first-class.
4. Marketplace colors/logos must not dominate Ordra's own identity.
5. Status and COD information must be recognizable at a glance.
6. Accessibility and readable contrast take priority over decorative effects.
